# 0156 — Add-on 0.4.0: Save buttons on every site from install, and a simple popup

Date: 2026-10-16
Status: proposed — built for the owner's approval of `docs/design/extension-0.4/extension-0.4-review.pdf`;
nothing shipped or submitted. Builds on ADR-0069, ADR-0125, ADR-0153 and ADR-0154, which still
hold unless this record says otherwise. (The number is the one the brief gave; on 2026-10-16 no
branch had an 0155 either, and this work does not use it.)

## Context

The owner (2026-10-16) put our add-on 0.3.2 beside a competitor's web importer in Chrome:

- "I can see the [competitor's] button but I can't see an Add to Thesis button anywhere." Theirs
  shows an inline button on every site by default, with a per-site switch under the global one.
  Ours showed buttons on five sites only; everywhere else needed "Show Save buttons on every site",
  off by default and behind Chrome's permission prompt (ADR-0154).
- "Their UI/UX is good and simple; ours is not." Theirs: a header (logo, name, refresh, settings,
  collapse, close), the current page's title and author, the signed-in account with "Open …" and a
  "…" menu, and a separate settings panel (theme; inline buttons on all sites, plus the current
  site; version). Ours opened on a long "No paper on this page" block with a bulleted list, had
  "Open app" in the header and the every-site switch under the main view.

## Decision

1. **Buttons on every site, from install.** The manifest is now built from `src/manifest.ts`
   (tested in `test/manifest.spec.ts`): `host_permissions` holds `https://*/*` beside the API's
   host, `optional_host_permissions` is gone, and a second `content_scripts` entry runs the same
   `content.js` on `https://*/*` in the top frame at `document_idle`, with `exclude_matches` for the
   five sites (they keep their own entry) and for Thesis Copilot's host and its subdomains.
   `neverHere` in `content.ts` and `findSpots` still refuse our own site a second time. What the
   script reads and sends is ADR-0154's, unchanged: article tags and reference-list DOI links,
   nothing sent until a press. The service worker answers a content script from any https tab
   but our own (`senderMay` with `anywhere` always passed); the 403 "turned off" answer is gone.
   0.3.2's run-time registration (`tc-anywhere`) is removed on update (`retireDynamicScript` in
   `onInstalled`); the page flag would stop a second run anyway.
2. **Off is a setting, not a permission.** `src/settings.ts`: `inlineButtons` in
   `chrome.storage.local` = `{ all, offSites }`, missing or malformed read as on. Per-site keys are
   the host in lower case without `www.`; at most 500. The content script reads it before putting
   anything in and follows changes live (`followSettings`): turned off, the buttons and card are
   taken out of the open page (`removeInpage`); turned on, they come back, no reload. Never sent
   anywhere. The access itself stays, so the store warning is the broad one (see Consequences).
3. **The theme**, `theme` = system/light/dark in `chrome.storage.local`, applies to the popup
   (`data-theme` on `<html>`, the dark tokens under `:root:not([data-theme=light])` in the media
   query and under `[data-theme=dark]`) and to the in-page buttons and card (`data-theme` on their
   shadow hosts, `:host(...)` rules).
4. **The popup, redrawn** (`static/popup.html`, `popup.css`, `src/popup.ts`; `state.ts` unchanged):
   - header: the LogoMark and "Thesis Copilot", then icon buttons refresh (reloads the window, so
     the tab is read again), settings (gear) and close;
   - a paper found: its title, authors · year, venue (no DOI line, no eyebrow unless it came from
     a link or a PDF), the thesis and collection as two compact label/select rows, and one big
     "Save to Thesis Copilot". Saving: the same button, "Saving…" with a spinner. Saved: one
     line, "✓ Saved · Open in Thesis Copilot" ("In your library · …" when already there), with
     the thesis (and collection) in a small line under it and any PDF or collection problem as one
     short line;
   - no paper: one line, "No paper found on this page", and a small "How it works" link that
     opens one short paragraph — no list. Chrome's own pages and a robot check get their own
     one-liners the same way;
   - a results page: one line, "N papers on this … page", and "Select several (N)", which opens
     0.3.2's ticked list, pickers and "Save n to Thesis Copilot";
   - the account row at the bottom: an initial, the student's name (or address) and "Signed in",
     "Open Thesis Copilot", and a "…" menu headed by the full address, with "Sign out…", "Help"
     (`/help`) and "Report a problem" (`/contact`). Signed out: "Not signed in" and "Sign in".
     Who is signed in comes from Better Auth's own `GET /api/v1/auth/get-session` (new `account`
     request, popup only — a content script may not ask it). The address sits in the menu, not the
     row, because a typical address does not fit beside the button without being cut;
   - settings, in place of the main view: Theme (System / Light / Dark); Save buttons in pages —
     "On all sites" and "This site: host" (off and greyed when all sites are off, or the tab is
     not an https page); Keyboard shortcut (the one Chrome assigned, "Change"); Version.
5. **One wording.** The in-page button says "Save to Thesis Copilot" ("Saved" after saving, "In
   your library" when the lookup found it there); the compact chip beside a reference DOI stays
   the mark plus "Save"/"Saved". The card's button and the right-click item say "Save to Thesis
   Copilot" too.
6. **Authors given name first.** Publishers write `citation_author` as "Raman, Kavya", and a list
   of them read as four people ("Raman, Kavya, Iyer, Sanjay"). `givenFirst` turns a name with
   exactly one comma into "Kavya Raman" before the byline (and the reference line sent to resolve)
   is made. `paper.spec.ts`'s Nature example pinned the old text; its expectation now says
   "Yann LeCun, Yoshua Bengio, Geoffrey Hinton", on purpose.

## Not done

- **Sign out from the add-on itself.** Better Auth checks a POST's `Origin` against
  `trustedOrigins` (`env.APP_URL`), and a service-worker request carries the add-on's
  `chrome-extension://` origin, so "Sign out…" opens the theses page, where the button is. Making
  the add-on sign out would mean trusting its origin in the API — an API change left out here.
- **"Latest version".** The settings show the version only; the add-on has no way to know the
  store's latest without asking, and Chrome updates store installs itself.
- **The in-page card** keeps its 0.3.2 layout (record, facts, pickers, notices); only its button
  wording and theme changed.
- **A real profile.** The review set and the fresh-install check ran in Playwright's Chromium with
  fixture pages; the owner's reload of `apps/extension/dist` on live sites is the real check.

## Consequences

- **The install warning grows** to Chrome's broadest ("Read and change all your data on all
  websites"), and a 0.3.x install is disabled on update until the student accepts it. The store
  review is likely longer and may ask for the justification (STORE.md has it, PUBLISHING.md says
  so). A student who wants none of it turns the buttons off in Settings; the access stays.
- `STORE.md`, `PUBLISHING.md`, `README.md`, the privacy page (`/privacy`, the add-on section) and
  the store pictures say 0.4.0.

## Evidence

- `pnpm --filter @tc/extension test`: 16 files; new `settings.spec.ts` (fresh install on,
  everywhere/one-site off and back, `www.` folding, the cap, the theme, a page following the
  setting live and on unreadable storage) and `manifest.spec.ts` (host permissions, no optional
  permission, both content-script entries, our own site excluded, the version);
  `anywhere.spec.ts` updated (excludes, the 0.3.2 registration removed, who is answered —
  never `account` from a page); the label expectations in `inpage`, `sbs-fixes` and `anywhere`.
  `typecheck`, `build` and Biome pass.
- `node apps/extension/scripts/review.mjs` (real Chromium, the built add-on, fixtures and a local
  API stand-in): the popup in every state, light and dark, and the button on Google Scholar,
  PubMed, an MDPI article (and "Saved" after saving) and Wikipedia references, in
  `docs/design/extension-0.4/` and `extension-0.4-review.pdf`; no page scrolled sideways.
- The fresh-install check in the same script: the production build in a new profile, nothing
  stored and no switch pressed — `https://*/*` held at install; one button on an MDPI article,
  one on a journal on no list (by its citation tags), three on the Wikipedia references, none on a
  page with no paper and none on Thesis Copilot's own page carrying article tags
  (`docs/design/extension-0.4/fresh-install.json`).
